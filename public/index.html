A Structured Pathway Into Cybersecurity

The Cybersecurity Foundation Program provides a clear, step-by-step pathway that helps professionals transition from survival jobs into meaningful cybersecurity careers.

Phase 1: Foundation Course (6 weeks)

1

Phase 1: Foundation Course (6 weeks)

A comprehensive 6-week foundation across six modules — from the threat landscape and vulnerability management to risk, cyberdefense controls and the cybersecurity workforce — with hands-on labs in tools like Qualys, Maltego and SpiderFoot.

Phase 2: Internship Program (12 weeks)

2

Phase 2: Internship Program (12 weeks)

Join the virtual internship at Hacktales Academy: hands-on technical training, guided classes and role-specific mentorship. Spend three months interning in one of our core teams — GRC, Security Engineering (Blue Team) or Offensive Security (Red Team).

Phase 3: Capstone Project (4 weeks)

3

Phase 3: Capstone Project (4 weeks)

Your final proving ground: solve a real-world cybersecurity challenge from start to finish with mentor feedback. The project becomes part of your portfolio — verifiable, employer-ready evidence of your skills.

Phase 4: Recruitment Support (2 weeks)

4

Phase 4: Recruitment Support (2 weeks)

Prepare to compete for cybersecurity roles in the job market. In this phase, we help you with:

  • Resume optimization
  • LinkedIn positioning
  • Mock interviews
  • Strategic job search guidance

Choose Your Track

In your chosen specialization, you go deep in one of three tracks — each preparing you for a different family of cybersecurity roles.

Blue Team

Learn how defenders monitor environments, identify suspicious activity, investigate security events and respond to threats. Practise the thinking and workflows used across security operations, vulnerability management and defensive security.

Best forSOC, security operations, vulnerability management, incident response and other defensive roles.

Red Team

Learn how attackers think and how security weaknesses are discovered, validated and communicated. Build practical exposure to offensive security concepts, testing methodology and responsible security assessment.

Best forPenetration testing, vulnerability assessment, offensive security and related technical roles.

GRC

Learn how organizations translate cybersecurity risk, regulation and business requirements into policies, controls and decisions. Practise risk assessment, compliance thinking, control evaluation, documentation and stakeholder communication.

Best forGovernance, risk, compliance, audit, privacy and security assurance roles. Great for career switchers with professional experience.
