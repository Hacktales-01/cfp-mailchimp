03 — Choose your track

Three tracks. One standard:
can you do the work?

Blue Team

Learn how defenders monitor environments, identify suspicious activity, investigate security events and respond to threats.

Best forSOC, security operations, vulnerability management, incident response.

Red Team

Learn how attackers think and how security weaknesses are discovered, validated and communicated responsibly.

Best forPenetration testing, vulnerability assessment, offensive security.

GRC

Learn how organizations translate risk, regulation and business requirements into policies, controls and decisions.

Best forGovernance, risk, compliance, audit and privacy. Great for career switchers.
